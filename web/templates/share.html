About public pages

  • Public pages are read-only — no editing capabilities are exposed.
  • Links to other wiki pages are displayed as plain text.
  • Encrypted (secure) fields are hidden on public pages.
  • The page is rendered without sidebars or navigation menus.
  • Images shown on the page load through the public link, without logging in. Other uploaded images stay private.
  • Chat apps such as Discord show a preview with the title, the start of the text and the first image. They may keep it for a while after you revoke the link.
